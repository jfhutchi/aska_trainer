# 0.1.7 gameplay-assist acceptance

Target: locally installed ASKA Steam build 25326768 / 1.44.1509261752._PC.Release,
Unity 6000.3.12f1 and the existing BepInEx IL2CPP installation. These steps describe
required manual acceptance; they are not completed gameplay results.

## Automated and source evidence

The integrated Release build passes with zero warnings/errors against the local
generated interop. The full core suite passes 220 cases (189 existing plus 11
fishing, 8 mushroom and 12 recruit cases). The GUI gate finds 78 reachable managed
IMGUI methods without unstripping-failure stubs. Existing regression checks still
reject the withdrawn building-work and item-decay/wear hook implementations.
Native fishing asset/weight/timer/catch paths, three mushroom resource identities
and weather dispatch, and recruit generation/selection/persistence were inspected
against build 25326768. These results do not verify live native detour execution.

## Fishing

1. Open Fishing & Foraging. Start at normal bite speed/rare weight with Easy Catch
   off, use a working rod/bait/location and complete an ordinary cast and catch.
2. Select 2x then 4x bite speed on successive casts. Compare waiting times over
   several bites, keeping the same bait/location/world time. Fish still require
   normal casting and reeling. Do not compare one random wait as proof of a ratio.
3. Enable Easy Catch. Confirm a longer opportunity to react and successful valid
   catches. An empty or invalid cast must not create items or progress.
4. Select 4x Rare Fish Boost where an eligible special fish is available. Confirm
   ordinary catch, bait consumption and fishing progress still occur. A few casts
   cannot establish a statistical probability; the label is a relative weight.
5. Return all fishing settings to normal/off, then test another cast. Unequip/drop
   the rod and change worlds to check that the effect follows only the current
   local player and leaves no permanent configuration edits.
6. Confirm Nice Catch and Fisherman through their normal in-game conditions.
   The trainer does not write platform achievements or progress counters.

## Mushrooms

Use existing depleted mushroom patches and the feature's documented eligibility
and timing rules. Compare normal and accelerated regrowth under the same weather
and world customization. Default availability-only mushrooms use a two-game-day
reference: 2x allows one-day replenishment and 4x allows half-day replenishment,
subject to the native weather/season gates. A finite existing interval is divided
by the multiplier; season-only and never-regrow configurations remain native.
Gather the mushrooms yourself and inspect Mushroomed
progress. Check that nearby trees, berries, ore and other resources retain their
normal behavior. Disable, change worlds and save/reload to check scheduling and
ownership cleanup. Do not infer achievement progress from item quantity alone.

## Recruit traits

1. Open Tribe and refresh recruit choices at an owned, idle recruitment outlet.
2. Choose an ordinary unsummoned candidate. Record its name, appearance and traits.
3. Generate a reroll preview. Confirm the pending candidate has not changed until
   Apply is selected. Refresh or Reset All should discard transient previews.
4. Apply the preview, reopen the normal recruitment selection if necessary, and
   confirm its name/appearance are retained and the displayed traits match.
5. Summon normally and confirm that the new villager has those traits. Confirm
   normal membership, AI, assignment and persistence after save/reload.
6. Change the pending choice, begin a summon or dismantle the outlet between
   preview and Apply. A stale preview must be rejected; no other candidate or
   established villager may receive its traits.
