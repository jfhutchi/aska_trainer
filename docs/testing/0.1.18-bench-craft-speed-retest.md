# HutchASKA 0.1.18 bench crafting retest

Bench Craft Speed is in F8 > Crafting & Building. Its 1x/Normal and 3x buttons
apply to the local player and current owned villagers at crafting benches. The
status line reports boosted player and tribe progress updates. Other crafting
and construction controls are independent.

1. With other crafting and speed options off, craft a known bench recipe at 1x.
   Note its approximate work time and ingredient/product counts.
2. Craft the same recipe at 3x. Confirm the player update counter increases,
   work finishes sooner, and the normal ingredient/product counts remain.
3. Assign a current owned villager the same or another repeatable bench recipe.
   Compare 1x and 3x, and confirm the tribe update counter increases.
4. Return to 1x, craft again, and confirm normal work time. On a new world load,
   confirm the option is disabled until explicitly selected.

Gameplay acceptance remains pending. A build and interop check do not prove
that ASKA advances progress through these session updates in a live save.
